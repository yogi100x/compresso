## Root cause and required practice

Nearly every PARTIAL above shares one cause: **remediation was written as an "Audit amendments" overlay instead of editing the authoritative ticket/spec bodies.** Amendments that contradict un-edited bodies are worse than either alone — two sources of truth, opposite contracts (E5-06 eager-vs-lazy is the sharpest case: the body's test asserts the amendment's behavior FAILS). Required practice going forward: amendments may announce a change, but the body text must be edited in the same pass; grep for the superseded wording before closing.
